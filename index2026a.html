📖 Course Description

Finance A is an introductory course at the College of Law and Business Studies in Ramat Gan, Israel. We cover time value of money, risk and return, investment valuation, and fundamental corporate finance principles.

👨‍🏫 About the Lecturer

Dr. Shay Tsaban is a senior lecturer specializing in accounting, finance, behavioral finance, economics, and decision-making. Contact: shay.tsaban@pac.org.il.
