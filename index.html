Course Description

Finance A is an introductory course at the College of Law and Business in Ramat Gan, Israel. We cover time value of money, risk, investment valuation, and basic corporate finance — practical tools for sound financial decisions.

About the Lecturer

Dr. Shay Tsaban is a senior lecturer in accounting, finance, behavioral finance, economics, and decision-making. His PhD research focuses on the economics of happiness. Contact: shay.tsaban@gmail.com.
